# Writing style

Every line of prose in this game is written in one voice. Hold new and edited
text to it.

## The rule

Plain, concrete, declarative. Short sentences. Real nouns and active verbs.
State the fact and stop. The dread, the grief, the strangeness live in what
happened — not in how the narrator describes it. The reader feels more when you
explain less. (Hemingway's iceberg: most of it is under the water, and you trust
it to hold the weight without pointing at it.)

The clinical voice already in the game is the model. Keep writing like the log:

> 0552: subject woke. remained in bed eleven minutes. classification unchanged.

Flat, exact, and far more unsettling than any sentence that reaches to unsettle.

## Do

- One idea per sentence. Cut the second clause if the first carries it.
- Concrete nouns: bed, glass, key, coat. Plain verbs: woke, drank, walked, initialed.
- Keep load-bearing repetition. "the handle is warm" earns its returns because
  it is plain. A motif is fine; a motif that admires itself is not.
- Let dialogue be spoken, not composed. People say things; they do not author them.
- Trust the stop. A short node that ends early beats a long one that explains.

## Don't — forbidden language

These are the tells of the voice straining. Cut them.

- **Reaching similes and metaphors.** "a sound like a held breath let go two
  rooms away", "like weather on a roof", "like wallpaper in an old house", "a
  breath like a hand held out through glass". If a simile decorates instead of
  clarifying, delete it.
- **Editorial twist-closers.** The clever last clause that tells the reader how
  to feel: "which should not be a feeling and is one", "honest as ever",
  "load-bearing as ever", "the difference is company", "which is how the room
  side says forever". End on the fact, not the flourish.
- **Abstraction worn as mystery.** "the wrongness is load-bearing", "the kept
  middle", "the room breathing", "you are the cabinet", "you carry your own eyes
  past it". Name the concrete thing instead.
- **Precious knowingness.** "you are done pretending not to recognize", "some
  dates deserve to be found again", "a hand that writes ahead of you", "it is a
  tucking in".
- **Mystery by withholding the plain word.** Do not riddle when the plain
  statement is stranger: "they stop being letters you recognize and start being
  yours anyway" → say what is on the page.
- Adverb reflexes (-ly stacked on verbs that already carry it), "suddenly", and
  piled adjectives.

## The test

Read the line aloud. If it sounds like it is performing, it is wrong. If it
sounds like a tired person telling you exactly what happened, it is right.

## Names

Real names for human characters; fantasy names only for creatures and aliens.
Places are exempt. Surnames are welcome.
