#+title: Rogue Branch

* Status

Stub branch with several implemented nodes.

* Entry

The upper dream maze exit leads to a Rogue-inspired dungeon path.

* Author truth

- The branch should evoke terse dungeon logic: inventory, doors, hidden danger,
  identification, stairs, and grid-like movement.
- It should be austere and readable, not a parody.
- Earlier room details can return as dungeon objects, but should feel like they
  were always part of this frame.

* Current surface

- =rogue/entrance=
- =rogue/inventory=
- =rogue/door=
- =rogue/loot=, where wearing the ring sets =rogue-ring-worn=
- =rogue/stairs= descends past the carved name; turning back finds the door
  locked from the other side.
- =rogue/floor-two= and =rogue/cell= end in a room furnished like the base
  bedroom, with no lock plate on the inside.
- =rogue/pillow= hides a half-used matchbook; =rogue/sleep= fades out with
  the torch.

* Delve device

The south door off the second-floor landing opens the long stair: the
=:rogue-delve= minigame (=game/rogue/delve.lisp=), the path's central
device.

- Reusable: maps, save prefix, and outcome ids all arrive via node
  config. Other paths or mods can run their own delves with their own
  floors.
- Tiny black-and-white pixel sprites (5x5 bitmaps), a four-tile torch
  radius, turn-based steps: player moves, then the hunter answers.
- All progress (floor, position, marks, hunter, picked set) lives in
  the dialog store under the prefix, so the autosave keeps mid-delve
  progress and the stair resumes where the player left it, even across
  restarts.
- Marks gathered echo the chalk tally; three or more change the bottom
  reveal. The bottom room is the bedroom again, with a fresh RETURN IF
  FOUND tag on the bed. Being caught wakes the player on the landing,
  counted, nothing missing except the going down.
