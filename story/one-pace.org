#+title: One Pace From the Threshold

* Status

Loaded dogfood mod, now over the 10-minute typed floor.

* Entry

The mod grafts onto =base/listen/steps=, the hall-sound leaf where footsteps
stop one pace from the threshold.

* Author truth

- It should continue the same situation rather than announce itself as a side
  story.
- The visitor is not randomly chosen by the player. The player's stance decides
  how the already-present visitor is understood: former occupant, stranger, or
  someone whose claim cannot be cleanly proven.
- The room can belong to different people by route, but the reveal should feel
  like ownership was always contested rather than reality changing.

* Current surface

- =one-pace/landing= extends the stopped footsteps into a winter-night door
  vigil with snow particles and drone music.
- =one-pace/name= collects the name on the coat tag into =one-pace-name=.
- =one-pace/recognise= records whether the name is known in =one-pace-stance=.
- =one-pace/bolts= records =one-pace-bolts= and routes by delegate.
- =one-pace/brace= uses the mod-local brace minigame.
- The shared close now runs from =one-pace/dawn= through =one-pace/dawn-key=
  before returning to =base/awake=.

* Minigame

Kind: =:one-pace/brace=

- Hold space, W, or up arrow to brace the door.
- Success and failure route through stance-specific aftermath nodes.
