#+title: JRPG Adventure

* Status

Implemented as the largest current branch spine.

* Entry

The matchbook path leads into a straight-faced fantasy adventure. It should read
at first as a bog-standard JRPG demon-lord quest: inn, village, companion,
overworld, slime, tower, demon lord, and a return road.

* Author truth

- The familiar genre frame is useful because later details can pull certainty
  away.
- It can be corny and stereotypical early, but should not wink too hard.
- The demon-lord route is one built-in spine, not the canonical endpoint for
  all JRPG play.
- Hidden long-term idea: the demon lord may turn out to be the player from
  another path. Do not foreshadow this in the early route.
- Stats, route choices, companion choice, combat results, inventory, and
  village errands should live in the shared store for later hooks and mods.
- The companion route should come from ordinary actions the player takes before
  the gate, not from choosing who happens to appear.

* Minigames

** Overworld

Kind: =:jrpg-overworld=

Current behavior:

- WASD/arrows movement.
- Small map with village, bridge, sign, tower, grass.
- Reaching grass advances to the road mile marker.
- The same minigame is configured and reused for the return road.
- Movement stores route/landmark facts and per-road step counts.

Expansion direction:

- Larger Oakbarrow-to-tower map.
- More landmarks: shrine, pond, roadside camp, old bridge, toll hut.
- More walking is now viable because the path has ambience and short cues.

** Turn combat

Kind: =:jrpg-combat=

Current behavior:

- 2x2 menu: =ATTACK=, =MAGIC=, =ITEM=, =RUN=.
- Uses JRPG state for HP/MP/gold/potions/xp.
- Victory, defeat, and retreat set =jrpg-last-battle=.

Expansion direction:

- Add more enemies.
- Add companion effects.
- Add a separate battle cue only if it does not become too rhythmic.

** Interrogation

Helper: =dialog-interrogation=

Current behavior:

- Pell has the first JRPG question menu at =jrpg/pell-questions=.
- Questions are hidden after being asked by store flags.
- =:require-all t= holds the approach choice until all three practical
  questions have been asked.

* Dark branches

- =ledger/= (done, 20.0 min): Mira's other ledger read after breakfast as
  promised: names, years, left-behind, and the closed circle that farms
  heroes. Entered inline from =jrpg/terms-asked=; a traveler arrives for
  notice sixty-seven mid-read; rejoins at =jrpg/evening-table=. Keys:
  =ledger-question=, =ledger-decision= (jrpg store).
- =bellfall/= (done, 20.1 min): nine quiet years after the terms, the
  midnight bell, Vane's funeral on the hill, and the year the notices must
  start again. Gated on =jrpg-vane-answer= = terms via
  =jrpg-room-four-target=; jrpg-combat reused for the ditch; ends in the
  posting, the succession, or the hold. Key: =bellfall-choice=.

* Bright branch

- =festival/= (done, 17.5 min): the midsummer fair, with Vane down from the
  tower in a plain grey coat. Carries the seeded-random mechanic:
  =festival-seed= is rolled once into the store on entry and
  =festival-roll= (an avalanche hash, save-stable, version-stable) derives
  stall order, ring-toss prize, loaf guess, cup game, fortune, and whose
  lantern snags the oak. Entered from =jrpg/fair-choice=.
