#+title: War Leader

* Status

Partly implemented.

* Entry

The refuse-all route through door counting and hall sounds should lead to a
nation-leader-in-war story.

* Author truth

- The player is a head of state or equivalent national leader during a war.
- The opening room can later read as a bunker, command residence, cabinet room,
  or emergency office.
- The horror is abstract command: reports, maps, propaganda, incomplete
  information, and costs paid by unseen people.
- The player should not feel like a battlefield hero.

* Planned surface

- Door counting can become fronts, ministries, shelters, or briefings.
- Hall sounds can become radio static, boots, sirens, water pipes, blackout
  glass, archive keys, or distant artillery.
- The first clear address should be restrained, such as a title spoken by an
  aide.

* Current surface

- =game/campaign/war.lisp= grafts onto the static, bells, and glass
  hall-sound leaves. The other leaves stay open as hook points.
- Brandt is the aide; the first address is "chancellor", said in passing.
- =war/doors= reuses =door-count= as the cabinet room number.
- Olen runs the map. =war/decision= stores bridge/rail-yard/delay in
  =war-first-order=; each option's cost lands on unseen people.
- =war/radio= is the tuning minigame: the quiet band exists and is reading
  numbers too. Either outcome turns the radio down, not off, and sleeps.
