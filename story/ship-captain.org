#+title: Ship Captain

* Status

Partly implemented.

* Entry

The glass of water path leads into the hard-sci-fi ship captain story.

* Author truth

- The player is a stressed captain on a large hard-sci-fi ship.
- The captain is near a breaking point.
- The crew sees an impeccable captain who always makes the correct call.
- The player privately carries the failed attempts, pain, death, and memory.
- Crew praise should become painful because it only sees the final polished
  result.
- Hallucinations are memory intrusions from previous failures, not ghosts,
  prophecies, or reality changing in front of the player.
- Register: between dark Star Trek and outright space horror, mostly
  psychological. The crew is unaware of the horrors; the captain knows them
  and the gore and carnage they would cause, because in failed attempts they
  happened.
- Plan impossible choices where no option saves every crew member; store the
  result so later scenes remember who was lost.
- The ship's civilization is branch-relative canon: dystopic vestige of
  humanity, thriving empire, or a republic/alliance of planets, per route.
- Time-switch to an unknown future where the captain is alone aboard and does
  not remember why: all dead, all dropped off safely, or in between. Keep the
  ambiguity. Scene cuts use the =:scene= lower-third node.

* Current surface

- =ship/wake= reveals restraint straps and indicator lights in reflection.
- =ship/name= collects the name stenciled on the frame at the foot of the bed.
- =ship/flight= runs the crossing minigame; the fiction says crossing, not
  wireframe. The hull is a true 3d model that pitches and banks with input.
- =ship/later= opens the lonely-future interlude behind a lower-third scene
  shift.
- Failure returns to the same alarm without explaining the mechanism, and
  silently counts in =ship-failures=. The failure return uses the =:warp=
  particle field before fading back to stars on the alarm.
- =ship/bridge= and =ship/praise= introduce Imari (logs) and Voss (lanes);
  "you make it look rehearsed" is admiration meant to sting later.
- =ship/galley-remembered= is the first memory intrusion: Voss's burned
  sleeve mid-pour, shown only after at least one failed crossing.
- =ship/voss-question= records the captain's explanation; =ship/bunk= sleeps
  back into the room.
