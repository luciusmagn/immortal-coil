* Status

Partly implemented.

* Entry

The glass of water path leads into the hard-sci-fi ship captain story.

* Author truth

- The player is a stressed captain on a large hard-sci-fi ship.
- The captain is near a breaking point.
- The crew sees an impeccable captain who always makes the correct call.
- The player privately carries the failed attempts, pain, death, and memory.
- Crew praise should become painful because it only sees the final polished
  result.
- Hallucinations are memory intrusions from previous failures, not ghosts,
  prophecies, or reality changing in front of the player.
