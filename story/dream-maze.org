#+title: Dream Maze

* Status

Implemented as a Doom-like dream-branch surface.

* Role

Sleeping enters the dream maze. The maze is a branch surface for dream genres.
It should not explain that it is selecting a genre.

* Author truth

- Dream exits should become portals into other narrative frames.
- Exits should eventually be less mechanical and more tied to remembered
  detail, orientation, or repeated symbols.
- Failure/lost results should lead into authored content, not a literal loop
  back to the opening.

* Current surface

- =base/sleep= enters =dream/maze= directly. Do not add padding before the
  minigame.
- =dream/maze= runs the Doom-like maze minigame.
- Left exit leads to Alice.
- Upper exit leads to Rogue.
- Right exit leads through =dream/right-exit=, =dream/right-line=,
  =dream/right-window=, =dream/right-card=, =dream/right-threshold=, and
  =dream/right-knock= before containment grafts in.
- Lost/failure leads through =dream/maze-lost=, =dream/lost-wall=,
  =dream/lost-count=, =dream/lost-light=, =dream/lost-coat=, and
  =dream/lost-follow= before containment grafts in.

* Audio and visual

- CRT static gets stronger near exits.
- Footstep sounds play during movement.
